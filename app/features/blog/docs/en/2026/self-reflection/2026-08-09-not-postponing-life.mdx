For several years, there has been a fairly clear priority inside me.

**Financial strength.**

Simply put, I treated growing my assets as more important than almost anything else.

With more financial room, I would have more choices — and I wanted to bring that time forward as much as I could.

## When Financial Freedom Came First

That is why I kept paying attention to the crypto market as well.

In 2022 and 2023, when the Bitcoin cycle was closer to winter, I gathered what courage I had and invested more actively.

I hoped that by 2024 at the earliest, or at least around 2025, I might see some kind of result.

That result was never only about growing assets.

I thought that with more financial room, I would use my time more freely — and put that time into the work I truly wanted to do.

But the change I expected did not arrive in the period I had hoped for.

Even now, I am not in a place of real financial ease.

Still, I do not think of that time as a failure.

I am still hoping this year as well.

As long as the world does not abandon crypto as an asset class altogether, I think there is a real chance that the momentum built so far will gather strength again at some point.

That said, I also think some altcoins should be cleared out without attachment when the right moment comes.

## To Do the Work I Want to Do

For me, the most natural way to talk about self-realization is still development.

Using what I do best to build my own services, and working in the direction I believe in.

I have pictured that for a long time.

Because I also expected some results from investing, I made a bolder choice in 2025.

I accepted about a 20% drop in income and started working four days a week.

With the time left, I began studying AI more seriously, and spending more hours on AI-assisted development and personal projects.

I had been studying related things before that as well, but 2025 was clearly when I shifted the direction of my life a little.

After that, I built many things.

Personally, there was real progress, and I learned a lot as a developer.

But it is hard to say I produced meaningful results on the outside.

Lately in particular, one thing has been hitting me hard.

**No matter how well you build something, making the world notice it is a completely different problem.**

Being good at development and having people care were separate issues.

Building good features alone was not enough.

I am slowly learning that a service only becomes whole when it also includes showing it to people, explaining it, talking, and listening to their responses.

So now I am also reconsidering the development cycle I used to repeat so comfortably.

## A Little Further Inward

Looking back, love and relationships always seemed to come a little later.

If anything, even the people who used to be close felt as if they drifted farther away across 2021 through 2024.

Maybe that started much earlier.

About ten years ago, I remember telling myself something similar.

That I would remain in the minority, even if it meant becoming lonely.

I thought it was fine not to mix with many people, and that living a little as an outsider was also a way of being myself.

But now, after 2025 and into 2026, my thinking has changed a lot.

I do not want to become a complete insider.

Still, I have started to feel that moving a little further inward might be okay.

Living needs a certain amount of warm exchange and conversation.

And I feel I also need a heart that pays a little more attention to the people around me, and looks after them first sometimes.

This is not only about personal relationships.

In building a service and bringing it into the world, connection between people mattered in the end as well.

Perhaps the limits I felt at work and the shifts I began to feel in relationships are not entirely different problems.

## It Was Fine — Until It Wasn't

Love, I put off even longer.

I thought living alone was not so bad, and for quite a long time, being alone really was fine.

But this year, cracks began to show in that thought.

**It is not fine anymore.**

I find myself wanting to start again.

I no longer want to leave liking someone, expressing that feeling, and spending time together only as something for a distant future.

That does not mean it has to lead to a particular outcome.

I just want to open again a part of myself that I kept closed for too long.

After financial freedom.

After my work succeeds.

After everything is a little more stable.

I began to think that if I keep postponing things that way, I might make the important parts of life wait far too long.

## Nothing Is Finished Yet

Looking at myself now from three angles feels a little strange.

Financial strength.

Self-realization.

Love and relationships.

It is hard to say that any of them has borne fruit in the form I hoped for.

But that does not mean any of them has failed either.

Because I moved toward financial freedom, I studied investing and the market.

Because I wanted to do the work I care about, I chose a four-day week.

And with that time, I gained a lot of experience in AI and development.

Because I went through a stretch of distance from people, I also came to understand the value of relationships a little more than before.

Nothing is complete, but something clearly changed along the way.

And beyond those three, I should add one more.

**Health.**

In the end, no matter what you achieve, you cannot enjoy it for long without health.

It is so obvious that it is easy to forget, but I do not want to put this off either from now on.

## Living Inside This Life

Financial strength still matters.

Financial freedom is still a very important goal in the life I want.

And I believe I will get there eventually.

My desire to build my own services and work in the direction I choose has not changed either.

But something is a little different from before.

I do not want to freeze the rest of life until those things are done.

I want to meet people,

pay a little more attention to those around me,

try love again,

take care of my health,

and keep building what I want to build.

Not everything will change all at once.

Some things may take much longer than expected, and incomplete parts or hard moments can appear at any time.

Lately I feel a little more able to accept those things as part of life too.

In the end, what matters may simply be being grateful for what I have now, and for this moment.

Not keeping everything else on hold for only one goal, but living as fully as I can in the things I find important.

And moving forward with a lighter heart, without clinging too tightly to outcomes.

That is how I want to live now.
