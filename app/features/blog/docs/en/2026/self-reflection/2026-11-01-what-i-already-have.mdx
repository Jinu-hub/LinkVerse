# What I Already Have

October has ended, and November has begun.

Looking back, a lot happened in October.

It may not have been a month of big achievements. Compared with the past few months, it was a time when I slowed down a little.

I am still building personal services, but I could not put as much time into them as before.

The light exercise habit I had been keeping also became a bit less frequent, and the blog posts I had been writing steadily stopped for about two weeks.

In the past, I might have felt a little disappointed looking at a stretch like this.

I might have pushed myself harder — telling myself I had to keep moving forward, that I had to use my time more efficiently.

But this October felt a little different.

## A Little Slower, A Little Wider

Instead of writing blog posts, I spent time looking inward.

Sometimes I drove out just to get some air.

I went back to gatherings I had barely attended for years, and for a short while I stepped out of daily life and visited Korea.

In Korea, after a long time, I had a really good time.

Meeting people, laughing together, talking — that time felt really good.

Even without doing anything special, I was reminded how much joy moments like these can bring into ordinary days.

Looking back, this change did not suddenly begin in October.

Even before then, I had been slowly giving more attention not only to work and development, but also to taking care of myself, to relationships, and to the small pleasures of everyday life.

And in October, that shift seemed to settle in more naturally — and with more energy.

Of course, there are still many services I want to build, and goals I still want to reach.

Wanting to move toward them has not changed.

But now, I also want the other parts of life to grow alongside that process.

**Not putting the present on hold for the sake of some future achievement, but enjoying life as it is now while also building the future I want.**

The changes I have been making little by little now seem to be settling quietly into my days.

## Something New Is Starting at Work Too

There seems to be a welcome change coming at work as well.

Where I work now, I had a chance to talk about the role I would take on going forward.

From November, I will still focus mainly on the work I have been doing, but I expect to set aside some of my time for AI-related projects as well.

I will have to wait and see exactly what I take on and how much, but for me this feels like a pretty good direction.

For a while now, I have put a lot of personal time into building with AI — both development and services.

I have learned new technologies, tried them myself, and through trial and error built up experience that feels like my own.

Those experiences now have a chance to reach beyond personal projects and into actual work.

Of course, taking on a new role is no guarantee that everything will go smoothly.

But it is encouraging to see that what I have been doing is beginning to lead somewhere new.

The pace of personal service development has slowed a little, but the experience I have built up is still opening into new possibilities.

This, too, is one of the changes I feel grateful for.

## There Is No Future Where Only Good Things Happen

Lately I have been trying to steer my mind in a more positive direction.

Holding onto gratitude, sensing abundance, and picturing the future I want.

But along the way, one thought has shifted.

At first, I think I believed something like this:

**"Really good things are going to happen to me from now on."**

Even now, I still hope that many good things will come my way.

But when I think about it, good things and not-so-good things have always existed side by side.

There were joyful moments and disappointing ones. There were times I got the results I hoped for, and times I did not.

Going forward will probably not be all that different.

No matter how positively I live, not everything will go the way I want.

So what is the change I actually hope for?

Now I see it a little differently.

**Not a future where only good things happen, but a change in how I notice and make sense of whatever comes my way.**

Even in the same situation, how I feel and what I do afterward can shift depending on how I look at it.

When something good happens, I want to feel glad and grateful for it.

But I do not want to get so caught up in that joy that I miss other things that matter around me.

One thing going well does not make everything perfect, and one thing going poorly does not make my whole life unhappy.

When things are hard, I can feel that they are hard. When I am sad, I can be sad. There is no need to force those feelings into something positive.

What matters, I think, is not getting too absorbed in any one emotion, and looking at my life with a slightly wider view.

In joyful moments, enjoying that joy fully while also noticing the happiness nearby; in difficult moments, not forgetting the precious things that are still beside me.

**Maybe the positive mindset I am looking for is not waiting only for good things, but being able to notice and appreciate the many sides of life in any moment.**

## Abundance Was Not Only Waiting in the Future

For a long time I have dreamed of financial freedom, and of a future where the services I build are loved by many people.

I also dream of living comfortably with someone I love, doing the work I want to do, and sharing ordinary days of laughter and conversation with good people.

Imagining that future still brings me a lot of joy.

But recently another thought came to me.

Don't I already have a lot?

I can choose the work I want to do, learn new things, and make something with my own hands.

I can take care of my health through exercise, and when I feel like it, I can go somewhere I enjoy.

I can meet people again after a long time and talk, and sometimes sit quietly alone and sort through my thoughts.

Above all, I can picture for myself the kind of life I want to live going forward.

I have been working hard to achieve more, but maybe I had not been seeing clearly enough the value of what was already beside me.

Abundance does not seem to be a feeling you can only reach at some point in the future.

It was something I could find in my life as it is now.

**Abundance is not something that arrives only when you have more. It is something you can feel when you recognize what you already have.**

These days I am slowly beginning to understand that difference.

## The Future I Imagine Is Already Beginning

Of course, there is still a lot I want to achieve.

I will keep building new services, and keep working toward a better life.

I will keep imagining ordinary days with someone I love, and a life with more financial freedom.

But happiness does not begin only after all of that is in place.

Even in this moment, I can feel grateful, and I can feel abundance.

A lot happened in October.

Judged only by visible results, it may have been a month that moved a little more slowly than before.

But for me, it was a time when the changes already underway deepened a little more, and my attitude toward life became a bit more natural.

**I already have a lot, and the future I imagine is on its way to me.**

Before I know it, only two months of this year remain.

In those two months, there will surely be good things.

And there will be harder ones too.

But I want to meet every one of those moments with a little more ease, and live them with joy and happiness.

**Maybe that is the biggest task given to me for the two months left in this year.**

Thinking about it, it sounds simple enough — but putting it into practice is harder than it seems.

Still, let's try.

This year's biggest task: living with joy and happiness.
